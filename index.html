How to duel

Left thumb · walk

Push anywhere on the left half. Your duelist really takes steps with its feet, so sudden changes of direction can make it stumble.

Right thumb · sword

Touch to take the sword from guard, then drag to aim the tip anywhere in front of you: up, down, left, right. Drag to the middle to point at your opponent and stab. Swipe fast across to slash. Let go to return to guard.

Cutting

A fast edge cuts into a limb. The deeper it goes, the more damage. Go most of the way through and the limb comes off. A stab to the heart or head kills instantly.

Rounds

Wounds bleed. If both duelists end up dead it is a draw. Survive your own bleeding after your opponent falls to score. First to 3 wins.
